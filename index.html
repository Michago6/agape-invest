About Us

Agape Invest – Our Vision, Our Why & Our Model

🌟 Our "Why" – Why We Exist

Agape Invest was founded from a simple but powerful insight: What truly builds success in early-stage ventures is not just capital – it's people.

We exist to give entrepreneurs access to social capital: teams, expertise, guidance, and relationships. We want to break the barrier that so many entrepreneurs face:

  • They have an idea, but not capital
  • They have the will, but not the resources
  • They have a dream, but not a team

This is where Agape Invest steps in. We offer a structured path from idea to reality, where the entrepreneur is not alone – but surrounded by experienced people who build alongside them.

💼 Our Structure – What Agape Invest Is

Agape Invest is a parent company that starts and manages subsidiaries in partnership with entrepreneurs. We operate as a hybrid between an incubator, investor, and socially driven accelerator.

We:

  • Hire experts in key areas (marketing, tech, finance, creative production, etc.)
  • Build teams for entrepreneurs who otherwise can't afford to hire
  • Offer office space & infrastructure
  • Invest capital in early stages (Seed)
  • Sometimes acquire existing companies with strong growth potential

In short, we create social, intellectual, and financial capital to build sustainable, profitable, and ethical businesses together with committed entrepreneurs.

🔁 Business Model – 2-Step Model with Optional Levels

All entrepreneurs start with Step 1 and can then choose to continue to Steps 2 and 3 depending on their needs and agreement.

Step 1 – Consultation & Access to Team (Required)

  • The entrepreneur books a 1-hour consultation
  • Free for youth aged 18–25
  • 4000 SEK/hour for individuals over 26
  • During the meeting, we analyze the business idea, needs, and map the competencies required for the company to thrive
  • If mutual interest exists, the entrepreneur gains access to Agape Invest's operational team, acting as their own employees

We solve the "personnel issue" – without the entrepreneur needing capital to hire.

📌 In return:

The entrepreneur gives 10% of the shares in their company (X AB) to Agape Invest.

Step 2 – Seed Capital (Optional)

  • Agape Invest invests between 25,000 SEK – 100,000 SEK in the client's company
  • 25,000 SEK is often used to cover basic startup costs such as registering the company
  • The investment is not tied to the amount but always grants a fixed 20% ownership share

📌 In return:

Agape Invest receives 20% of the company's shares

💡 The capital is often used for:
  • Company formation
  • Product development
  • Marketing/test campaigns
  • Platform costs or MVP

📈 Acquisition Strategy – Scale Existing Companies

In addition to working with startups, Agape Invest also seeks established businesses for sale or in need of change.

Our goal is to:

  • Buy into or acquire companies with untapped potential
  • Scale them through our expertise and network
  • 10x their profitability through efficiency, new strategy, and cultural change

👥 Youth Program – Network of the Future

We offer free membership for youth aged 13–19.

🗓️ Every Sunday, youth can visit Agape Invest's office to:

  • See how a company operates
  • Learn about business culture, entrepreneurship, and investing
  • Build relationships with our founders, investors, and team

📚 The goal is education, inspiration, and social capital – not salary. Young people with relevant skills can contribute to projects and build their portfolios and experience.

🤝 Philosophy & Conditions

  • Agape Invest always acts as a partner, not an owner
  • We require annual dividends based on ownership (10%, 20%, 30%)
  • This secures cash flow to invest in new startups
  • We exit if the entrepreneur shows lack of commitment
  • If the entrepreneur plans to sell their company, Agape Invest has first right to buy their share – if we believe in continued growth

🧝 Our Values

  • Independence: Founders must always own the majority of their company
  • Transparency: Everything is done in consultation, never unilaterally
  • Sustainability: We build long-term and ethically grounded companies
  • Trust: We believe in people before business plans
  • Community: We're building a network – not just a portfolio
