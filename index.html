What We Do – Our Model

1. Vision Meeting – We Listen to Your Idea

A one-hour session where we get to know you, your dream, and your direction.

Cost:

Ages 18–19: Free

Ages 20–25: 5,000 SEK

Ages 26+: 20,000 SEK

Our goal? To see if we want to walk this journey together.



2. Access to Our Team – If It Clicks

If we believe in your vision, and you want to build with us, you can access Agape Invest’s full team and expertise.

You get:

  • Strategy & business development
  • Legal, corporate structure & finance
  • Branding & marketing
  • Social capital & networking

Compensation: 10% equity in your company.

Note: No capital included – this offer is for team, network, and resources only.



3. Team + Capital – When You’re Ready to Scale

Want both access to our team and seed funding to take the next step? We offer a flexible investment model.

Investment from Agape Invest Equity Taken What You Get
25,000 SEK 12.5% Team + Capital
50,000 SEK 15% Team + Capital
75,000 SEK 17.5% Team + Capital
100,000 SEK 20% Team + Capital

You get a committed partner who not only funds you – but builds with you.
